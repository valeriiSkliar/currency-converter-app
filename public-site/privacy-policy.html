Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026 | App: Convertoff Currency Converter (com.cimmetria.currencyconverter)

Summary: Convertoff respects your privacy. We do NOT collect, store, or sell any personal data. Camera access is used exclusively on your device for OCR price scanning and never leaves your phone.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Convertoff Currency Converter ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android and iOS. By using the app, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy.

2. Camera Access & Price Scanner (OCR)

Convertoff includes an optical character recognition (OCR) price scanner feature.

  • On-Device Processing: Camera access is required solely to capture price tags in real time. All image processing and character recognition happen locally on your mobile device.
  • No Data Retention or Transmission: Photos, video feeds, or scanned images are NEVER recorded, saved to storage, transmitted over the internet, or uploaded to any server.

3. Advertising & Analytics (Google AdMob)

The free version of Convertoff displays ads served by Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable information, such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general location, to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
  • User consent is requested via the User Messaging Platform (UMP) and App Tracking Transparency (ATT) in accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and Apple/Google guidelines.
  • You can view Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Upgrading to Convertoff PRO completely disables all advertising.

4. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions (Monthly and Yearly) are processed directly by the Google Play Store (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS). We do not collect, process, or store your credit card details or billing address. All financial transactions are managed securely by the store provider.

5. Exchange Rate Data

To provide accurate currency conversion rates, Convertoff fetches rate tables from our secure backend service (converter.firstbot.online). These network calls carry no personal identification tokens or user data.

6. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
